# Materials and collections

English | [Русский](DATABASE_MATERIALS.md)

Use **Database** to organize entries into collections. A collection can have its own fields, cover, and ratings. Open a collection to search and sort its entries and edit its settings.

You can keep a draft while editing an entry and attach reference files. Save the entry to keep its changes. To keep related work together, link an existing entry from a thought or project, or create a new entry there and link it back. Review links when removing an entry that other records use.

Create a [portable backup](BACKUPS.en.md) before moving data to another installation. A CSV export covers only part of your records.

The [Russian reference](DATABASE_MATERIALS.md) also records the development history of sound candidates and the checks performed in that iteration.
