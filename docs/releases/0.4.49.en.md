# Orvim 0.4.49

- Edit the calendar date and clock time of a meal in its editor, or ask the AI assistant to propose a correction to an existing entry. With a personal day starting at 05:00, a meal at 03:00 on November 20 counts toward November 19.
- On Android, tap to dismiss the system text selection menu or press Back. Long-press still selects text.
- Project materials start collapsed. New links, notes, and files are available in the project and under Archive → Project materials. Existing collections remain accessible.
- The database opens to a list of collections. Drag the handle on the right to reorder them. Open a collection to see its entries, covers, descriptions, and controls.
